# Sky Surveyor

A touchscreen drone-mapping mission game for the Oman Science Festival, for students aged 6–16, in Arabic and English.

Players join Maryam (GIS engineer) and Ahmed (drone pilot) for one mission over Salalah:

1. **Analyze**: tap the unknown objects to find the obstacles in the area.
2. **Plan**: shape the survey area, pick the altitude, and route around the obstacles, then press GO.
3. **Process**: solve the image puzzle to turn the raw photos into a map product.

Play it at https://nasiyba.github.io/sky-surveyor/ (works best in landscape on an iPad; use Share → Add to Home Screen for full screen).
